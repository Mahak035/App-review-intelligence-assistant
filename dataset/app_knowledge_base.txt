[3. KNOWN ISSUES MATRIX]
- Known Issue #101 (iOS 17 Camera Crash): Missing `NSCameraUsageDescription` string key in Info.plist combined with premature Metal C++ shader memory allocation causes immediate launch crash on iOS 17.
- Known Issue #102 (Battery Drain & Overheating): Camera story filters run un-throttled GPU compute loops on iPhone 15 Pro, driving CPU temperatures to 42 deg C.
- Known Issue #103 (Bluetooth A2DP Audio Lag): AAC audio codec negotiation fails on car stereo units, defaulting to 1000ms latency.
- Known Issue #104 (5G Feed Refresh Drop): Stale IPv6 DNS sockets cause 'Couldn't refresh feed' error when switching between Wi-Fi and 5G.

[4. FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS (FAQ)]
- FAQ 1: Why is my app crashing on iOS 17 after updating to version 4.1?
  Answer: Version 4.1 introduced a regression in the C++ Metal shader initialization and camera permission prompt. Update to patch v4.1.1 or re-grant camera permissions in iOS Settings -> Privacy -> Camera.
- FAQ 2: Which feature receives the most praise from users?
  Answer: The 120Hz ProMotion UI rendering pipeline and 4K 60fps video playback receive the highest praise (94% positive sentiment), closely followed by offline AI music pre-caching and instant UPI transfers.
- FAQ 3: How do I fix the 1-second Bluetooth audio delay?
  Answer: Toggle Bluetooth developer settings from AAC codec fallback to SBC/aptX HD.

[5. SUPPORT DOCUMENTS & ENGINEERING WRAP-AROUNDS]
- Support Doc #501 (iOS Camera Permission Fix): Verify that `NSCameraUsageDescription` is present in target Info.plist build settings before archiving IPA binaries.
- Support Doc #502 (Database Backup Unlock): Run `VACUUM;` command on `msgstore.db` sqlite database to clear lock files prior to Google Drive backup uploads.
- Support Doc #503 (5G Socket Reset): Execute `netsh winsock reset` or clear app network cache to purge stale IPv6 socket handles.
